# Gogh Strike

The pureTV edition adapts Peter and the Gogh Strike contributors’ game at commit `2285e640097d9b12bcc42af72d5e8d0b37082f51`. Their MIT license covers the game, procedural town, artist designs and original modifications to character models. A copy accompanies this game.

The twelve artist heads incorporate the official MakeHuman hm08 anatomical base and thirty morph targets under CC0 1.0 Universal. The bundled declaration and pinned source manifest are under `src/vendor/makehuman/`. The manifest records original asset URLs and hashes. Portrait cards render these models. No museum photographs or paintings are redistributed.

The packaged GLBs encode the twelve original heads with meshoptimizer 1.3.0, retaining both LODs, artist node names, skin materials and morph targets. Portrait cards and HUD faces are scaled WebP exports of the upstream renders. All four shared character textures are unchanged. The runtime MeshoptDecoder carries Arseny Kapoulkine’s MIT license.

The vendored Three.js 0.170.0 renderer and its addons carry the Three.js authors’ MIT license. The pureTV controller bridge carries the repository’s MIT license. The build includes these notices and the unabridged CC0 text in the offline distribution.
