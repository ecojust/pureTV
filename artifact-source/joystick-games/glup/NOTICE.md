# GLUP — original game and pureTV adaptation

Upstream: gavilanbe/glup, fixed commit db4bc2cf502b9b53bd06fe65491215ea2324ef82.
https://github.com/gavilanbe/glup/tree/db4bc2cf502b9b53bd06fe65491215ea2324ef82

Original game, nine levels, artwork and procedural music retained under the MIT license below. pureTV changes: independent nine-button input, explicit cancellation/pause, audio suspension, reserved controller sidebar, offline packaging and read-only QA. Original Spanish text retained. Browser service worker and automatic update checks are excluded from the offline package. Upstream simulation tools are kept for original regressions and are not exposed as a QA mutation interface.
