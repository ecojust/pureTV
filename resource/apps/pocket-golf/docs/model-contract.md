# Pure model and physics contract

Only `create(seed, level)`, `step(state, dt, input)`, and deeply frozen `config` are exported. Defaultseed793, level0. Public layouts contain only identity/names, start, cup, strokeLimit and AABB obstacles. No answer angles, route tables, random outcomes or test planners are shipped. Configuration and states are detached.

Coordinates:720×450; ball radius8, center bounds[8,712]×[8,442]. AngleIndex0..71, five degrees per index clockwise from right. Power1..5 selects initialspeed[80,155,230,305,380]; defaultangle0/power3. State includes ball{x,y,vx,vy,radius,moving}, cup{x,y,radius18,captureRadius12,maxSpeed65}, obstacles, angleIndex/power, strokes/strokeLimit, wallHits/obstacleHits, blockedActions, capture, physicsAccumulator/physicsTicks/shotTicks/shotTime, currentShot/lastShot/shotReceipts, trail, terminalReceipt, gate and timing counters.

At rest a fresh left/right direction changes angle by−/+1 modulo72; up increases power, down decreases; limits block freely. Fresh A starts a shot and consumes one stroke. B has no game action. Direction/A while moving is blocked, never queued; held A or held directions do not act on later settlement. Each shot is completed before the stroke budget can lose. A captured final allowed shot wins.

Physics uses a1/240s accumulator driven by admitted dt. No wall-clock manipulation or solver is part of the model. An admitted dt≤.25 creates at most61 fixed ticks including remainder. Each tick integrates constant deceleration95 exactly over `min(h,speed/95)`: displacement `(startSpeed+endSpeed)/2 * duration`, direction unchanged before contact; velocity never reverses due to friction. After cup/contact processing, speed≤8 becomes exact rest. Accumulator resets at launch/rest, so fully settled ordinary shots are invariant under input-frame partition. Trail stores at most80 samples at every12 internal ticks; receipts at moststrokeLimit.

Cup capture solves the actual segment/circle entry and exit interval. The interval must intersect the distance where `sqrt(startSpeed²−2*95*distance)≤65`. Thus an already fast crossing does not become valid merely because velocity later falls below65. Capture preserves actual crossingpoint/speed/stroke in state.capture; the displayed ball snaps to cup center and zero velocity. Low-speed capture occurs before contact correction; public cups have ample clearance from walls/obstacles. No angle/power alone can win. Final score100 for win,0 otherwise.

After free travel, outer bound overshoot is reflected in deterministic x-then-y order. Each contacted side multiplies both velocity components by0.9; a simultaneous outer corner applies twice. Obstacle contact uses nearest point on the rectangle to the circle center, including a geometric diagonal normal at rounded corners. Penetration is pushed out by depth+0.0001. Only an incoming dot product below−1e−9 reflects velocity and applies0.9 to both components. Outgoing overlap separates without another bounce. Three deterministic descriptor-order passes resolve simultaneous contacts; center is bounded afterward. Maxspeed380 moves≤1.584 units per tick, below ball radius and all obstacle thicknesses, preventing wall tunneling. Energy never increases. Authoritative geometry contains no narrow zero-width passage.

Receipt `{stroke,from,angleIndex,angle,power,speed,wallHits,obstacleHits,end,duration,result,capture}` records a settled shot (`rest`) or actual capture (`cup`). LastShot and bounded receipt copies are detached. Upon rest, remaining strokes allow another shot; otherwise phase lost/reason `strokes`. Capture finishes phase won first. TerminalReceipt snapshots outcome, ball, counts and receipts. All later calls freeze exactly. There is no countdown or forced timeout.

Input: own data descriptors only; finite clamped axes, literal booleans, dominant axis>.5 with vertical ties. FreshB>A>changednonneutraldirection priority, but B action is unused. Start disarmed and neutral arms; changed nonnull cancelEpoch or rising cancelled disarms until neutral. Cancellation suppresses controls while physics advances. Paused or invalid-time calls (not finite0<dt≤.25) are exact no-ops. Calls/events/lastCommand follow the common protocol; counters saturate2147483647 and elapsed86400. Test-only synthetic collision states are explicitly labelled and are separate from ordinary keyboard win/loss evidence.
