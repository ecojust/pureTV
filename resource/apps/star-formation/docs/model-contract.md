# Pure model and collision order

Only `create(seed,level)`, `step(state,dt,input)` and frozen `config` are exported. Defaultseed795/level0. Field720×450. Player center y420,30×18 rectangle, speed300, x clamped15..705, lives3, piercingLeft2. No vertical player movement. Public layouts are3×2,3×3,4×3 enemies; enemy34×24,1HP; column/row spacing92/44. Starting first-column x centers formation, first row y60. Every enemy retains stable ID/row/column and visible world x/y even after hp0. No random or hidden production policy.

Formation moves discrete28-unit horizontal steps (not continuous interpolation). Origin x is bounded using the current living extreme columns so every live enemy rectangle stays within side margins24..696. An attempted step past a side clamps to the edge, reverses direction and adds22 to origin y. Each count reduction immediately updates stepInterval to max(.12,base×(.45+.55×remaining/initial)), bases.34/.31/.28. stepClock and stepInterval are public. Shield cells overlapping a newly positioned live enemy are destroyed before bullets. Any live enemy bottom≥380 is invasion.

Enemy fire intervals1.25/1.1/.95s; speeds135/150/165. nextEnemyColumn rotates modulo layout columns, skipping empty columns; chosen source is the largest surviving row in the first available column. Enemies shoot vertically down from their bottom+6. Death of a source does not remove an already-fired projectile. Friendly speed620, shot center starts y405. All bullets are6×10 rectangles, stable IDs, y velocity, piercing flag and sourceEnemy ID/null. No bullet/bullet collision.

A held continuously fires when cooldown reaches zero, cooldown reset.18. Fresh B immediately commits one piercing round at the current player position and consumes one of two ammunition, even if dt is less than a fixed tick. There is no pending B queue to cancel. A and B supplement each other: an immediate B shot can be followed by normal A fire in the ensuing tick. Each side is capped at16 bullets; accepted ordinary gameplay remains well below the cap. At a full cap an attempted B does not consume ammunition. B held across calls/ticks fires only once.

Each fixed1/120s tick resolves:

1. Move player by admitted analog x, reduce immunity and normal fire cooldown.
2. Accumulate formation clock, perform due discrete steps/descent, crush overlapping shield cells.
3. Fire held A if ready; accumulate enemy clock and emit due round-robin bottom-column shots.
4. Process friendly bullets in ascending ID, then hostile bullets in ascending ID. Each vertical segment is swept against rectangles expanded by half bullet width/height. Candidate order is entry fraction, shield before enemy/player on ties, then target ID. Ordinary rounds stop at the first still-live object. Piercing friendly rounds destroy each crossed shield/enemy once in travel order and continue until leaving the field. Later bullets see earlier destruction. Enemy bullets hitting player are consumed; damage requires immunity≤1e−10 and lives>0, then gives.8s immunity.
5. Recompute remaining enemies/shields and acceleration. Terminal priority: remaining0 wins; otherwise lives0 loses `lives`; otherwise any live enemy bottom≥380 loses `invasion`. The win may therefore have lives0 in an explicitly simultaneous last-kill case. Subsequent calls freeze exactly.

Three arch shields centered180/360/540 have top4 and bottom2 outer cells. Cells18×14, horizontalpitch20, y330 and346,1HP. Both sides destroy ordinary cover on contact; piercing continues. Geometry is public and no answer path exists.

State includes player/lives/piercingLeft/invulnerable/fireCooldown; formation{x,y,direction,stepClock,stepInterval,steps,drops}; enemies/remaining/initialEnemies; shieldCells/shieldRemaining; friendlyBullets/enemyBullets; clocks/nextEnemyColumn/nextBulletId; shot and kill/damage counters. Bounded last32 shotReceipts record id/side/piercing/x/y/sourceEnemy/column/tick; last32 hitReceipts record bulletId/side/target/targetId/x/y/t/tick plus damage/crushed when relevant. Bullet receipts use actual contact position; crush receipts use cell center. Current events max32 reset per admitted call. TerminalReceipt is detached. Counters saturate2147483647; elapsed86400; all dynamic collections are bounded.

Admit finite0<dt≤.25, accumulate into fixed ticks. Own data descriptors only, finite axes clamped±1 and literal booleans. Start unarmed; neutral buttons and deadzone axes arm. Changed nonnull cancelEpoch or rising cancelled disarms control until neutral. Suppressed controls stop movement/A/B but physics continues, and held A cannot restart until the neutral release gate is satisfied. Already committed B bullets are physical objects and remain. Paused and invalid-dt calls are exact no-ops. Analog y has no movement action but participates in neutral detection. Browser QA uses real keyboard and wall-clock time, no model/clock injection.
