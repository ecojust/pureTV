Used Car Racing TV — original baked materials

All texture files in textures/ and the environment reflections generated by
materials.js are original project artwork. No photographs, extracted game
assets, commercial vehicle skins, or externally downloaded textures are used.

Reproduce the baked maps with:
  python3 tools/generate-used-car-textures.py
Dependencies: Python 3, Pillow, numpy. Runtime uses the bundled files offline;
the Android application does not require Python, a CDN, or a texture server.

Base-color maps are sRGB. Normal/specular maps contain linear data. Asphalt
and rock colors and the shared vehicle color atlas are 1024 x 1024; other
colors are 512 x 512. Supporting maps are 256 or 512 square. The vehicle
atlas packs paint, tire, lamp and cloth surfaces with wrapped edge gutters;
its normal and specular atlases are 512 square. textures/manifest.json
records the exact dimensions and normalized vehicle UV rectangles.
Tree silhouette is an alpha-tested original needle/branch drawing.
