# Art design — Fleet Ledger

Original maritime ledger on ivory sea-chart paper: unknown question marks, solid deck/porthole ship tokens and double-wave sea symbols. Adjacent ship squares have dark connectors so their maximal cardinal component is visible. Gold lock-shaped corners identify fixed clues independent of colour, with an explicit LOCK=FIXED CLUE legend.

The7×7 board uses48px cells; smaller boards55/64px. Row/column labels remain outside the grid, and current/target count ratios use measured text widths, including0 rather than omitting it. The ship-cell total displays two-digit11 correctly. Required fleet lists every length, including repeated lengths, rather than only total area.

The right panel separates live unknown/ship/check counts from last validation. Free incomplete hints, charged complete checks and stale reports have distinct headings. Six predicate labels show rows,columns,line,gap,fleet,full with OK/FAIL text as well as colour. Purple cursor remains visible on both editable and fixed cells.

Atlas art and SVG/Android icons are original vector work; no external imagery or answer grid. Custom slots avoid reserved renderer core/glyph regions. Actual960/1280 visual inspection, ordinary-input recording and final source/asset matching remain separate QA evidence; this design note does not claim hardware or browser acceptance.
