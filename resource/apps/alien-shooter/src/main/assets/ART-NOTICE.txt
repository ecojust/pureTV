Alien Shooter TV — original visual assets

All models, procedural textures, effects and logo in this application were
created for this independent PureTV game. No original game's sprites, maps,
models, recordings, logo or ROM are bundled.

renderer.js generates the following local CanvasTextures once at startup:
- Three seeded 1024 x 1024 steel-floor materials, with plate seams, fasteners,
  vents, scratches, surface noise and oil stains.
- A 512 x 512 brushed-metal surface and a 512 x 512 mottled organic skin.
- 128 x 128 contact shadow, light glow and splatter masks.
- A 1024 x 512 industrial floor-stencil atlas.

Low-poly models are built from authored arrangements of primitive geometry.
Static scenery is merged by material. Characters, separate gait parts, effects,
decals and pickups use bounded instanced pools. Textures and geometry are local;
no network image or model downloads are required.
