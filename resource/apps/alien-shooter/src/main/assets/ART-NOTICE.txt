Alien Shooter TV — original visual assets

All models, procedural textures, effects and logo in this application were
created for this independent PureTV game. No original game's sprites, maps,
models, recordings, logo or ROM are bundled.

The offline recipe tools/bake-materials.mjs creates the bundled baked textures:
- A 1024 x 1024 padded material atlas with authored cargo-crate bevels, seams,
  straps, bolts, labels and wear; metal, organic skin, barrels and pickup marks.
- A shared 1024 x 1024 steel-floor material, with seams, fasteners, vents,
  scratches, surface noise and stains. Per-stage tint avoids three full copies.
- Three 512 x 512 map-space, linear-RGB ground-light maps containing approximate
  fixed contact occlusion, authored colored light pools and floor markings.
  These are distance/visibility-based ground approximations, not path-traced
  lighting. Dynamic doors, breakable barrels and actors are not baked into them.
- Original block-letter glyphs are drawn by the recipe; no system font or
  third-party image is needed. Atlas/floor PNGs use 256-color encoding; ground
  lighting retains RGB. PNG dimensions and source hashes are checked in CI.

Small contact-shadow, light-glow and splatter masks remain local procedural
textures. Baked files are decoded before rendering, with no network downloads.

Low-poly models are built from authored arrangements of primitive geometry.
Cargo-crate surface detail lives in the atlas instead of many tiny solid parts.
Static scenery is batched; animated characters, effects, decals and pickups use
bounded instanced pools. Textures and geometry are local. See docs/baking.md for
the bake contract, reproduction instructions and honest memory accounting.
