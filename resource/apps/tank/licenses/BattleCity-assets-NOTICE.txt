Native sprite data in src/main/assets/battle-art.js is derived from the upstream
images/*.png collection. Files in src/main/assets/audio/*.ogg are copied exactly
from the upstream sound/*.ogg collection. Per-file source hashes and conversion
notes are in docs/reference-art-manifest.json and reference-audio-manifest.json.

Battle City (1985), its original title, artwork designs, and sound design are
credited to Namco. This project does not claim authorship of Namco's original
work. The upstream repository supplies no separate asset-specific authorship
records. Its MIT license is retained as supplied, without asserting ownership
over the original game's artwork or compositions. This is an unofficial,
independently written recreation, with no ROM or emulator runtime.

The JavaScript renderer, font, ice approximation and synthesized engine texture
were written for this project. See docs/reference-art-audio.md for the known
fidelity limits and attribution details.
