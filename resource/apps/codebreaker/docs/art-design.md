# Codebreaker art direction

Original engraved cipher-box artwork pairs a warm parchment guess ledger with a dark green editing console. Every symbol has an independent shape and a stable number1–6; color is supplementary. The six shapes are circle, triangle, square, diamond, pentagon and star.

The left ledger keeps all nine possible attempts visible at once, with four compact shape/number pairs and two aggregate feedback columns E/M. Feedback does not color individual slots or imply per-position correctness. Lower-budget rounds gray out unavailable row numbers. The latest submitted row has a thin brass outline. Editing the current guess never rewrites ledger entries.

Four large editing keys occupy the right panel. A brass frame identifies the selected slot, blank slots show a question mark, and the palette lists only symbols available in the current round. Remaining attempts and submission readiness remain visible. Terminal rounds may show the revealed code; playing states never receive or render it.

All stage text is at least24logical pixels. Nine history rows use36pixel pitch, preserving readability at the smaller viewport. The game paints an original desk, six symbolic glyphs and a keycap once into the common atlas, then uses sprite batching. SVG and Android icons use matching decorative shapes rather than any game's secret code.

This design is not a claim of completed browser, video or physical-device testing; actual review is documented separately.
