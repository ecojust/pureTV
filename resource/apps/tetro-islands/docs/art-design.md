# Art and interaction

Original muted chart-paper board with dark glazed stone black marks, cream hollow white markers, question marks, rose cursor and fine gold selected-region interior outline. Region borders are3px and IDs are repeated at20px in every cell, so region membership never relies on tint alone. Number remains cream on black tiles. The7×7 board uses52px cells; main state marks28px and coordinates22px. Repeated IDs and current-region sidebar keep large irregular regions traceable.

I/L/T/S miniatures show free shapes, with Chinese rules explaining mirror/rotation equivalence and forbidden O. Current region count and type are separate from global check results. Six report predicates use explicit OK/FAIL labels; free incomplete checks, charged complete checks and edited stale reports have separate headings. Header/sidebar/footer text is width-fitted to fixed available areas. White cells have no continuity rule.

Atlas tiles are original Canvas2D paint primitives at0..256×128, away from reserved disc/white/glyph rectangles. No external or licensed stock images. Icon is a decorative abstract partition with three illustrative shapes, not a puzzle solution. The SVG and Android vector are authored in this app directory.

Actual browser images at960×540 and1280×720, mark/region-label legibility, and source/asset hash consistency still require the serialized QA preview. Static source dimensions are not visual acceptance evidence.
