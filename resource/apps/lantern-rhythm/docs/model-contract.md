# Lantern Rhythm model contract

UMD `PureModel` exports only `create(seed=1,difficulty=0)`, `step(state,dt,input)` and `TICK_HZ=60`. State is serializable; the model mutates it at fixed 1/60 s ticks and has no forced-result, scoring override, or solution API. Three authored levels each contain three phrases, with 3, 4, or 5 notes per phrase. They are named `港口三拍`, `海湾四拍`, and `远礁五拍`.

## Public state and visibility

- `width=960`, `height=540`, `level` 1…3, `difficulty` 0…2, `levelName`; `phase` is `playing`, `won`, or `lost`.
- `mode` is `listen`, `count-in`, `respond`, or `feedback`. `phraseIndex` is zero-based, `phraseCount=3`, `phraseLength` 3/4/5, and `answered` is the number of accepted notes in the current attempt.
- `beacons[]` contains three `{id,x,y,lit}` entries at (280,438), (480,456), (680,438). `litCount` is the number of fully matched phrases. `correctNotes` counts notes in lit phrases plus the current unfinished phrase's accepted prefix. A completed phrase contributes only once.
- `visibleCue` is null except during an actually visible current listening cue. It contains `{index,direction,kind,duration,startedAt,active}`. Directions are `up`, `right`, `down`, `left`; kinds `short`, `long`. A cue's identifying arrow and short/long label remain visible for `cueDisplayDuration=1.05` s, while `active` describes its actual .30/.72 s flash. There is never a public array of future directions or durations.
- Listening uses `listenSerial`, `listenStartedAt` (first cue onset), and `listenEndsAt` (end of the last beat). Each new/repeated listening pass increments `listenSerial`. `visibleCue` disappears during every response/count-in/feedback interval. Fields beginning `_` are private expected-pattern/input state and must never be read by renderer, pilot or gameplay UI.
- Response timing is public: `onsets[]` contains absolute model `elapsed` timestamps only; `responseStartAt`, `nextOnset`, `countInRemaining`, `beatPeriod=1.5`, `onsetTolerance=.22`, `countInDuration=1.8`. No public response field identifies a note's expected direction or short/long kind. `nextOnset` is for index `answered`, or null after the phrase is complete. The model becomes `respond` when the first unanswered note's early window opens; countdown may still be positive during that early window.
- `shortDuration=.30`, `shortMin=.14`, `shortMax=.46`, `longDuration=.72`, `longMin=.56`, `longMax=.88` are public rules, not note answers. The two accepted hold ranges are disjoint and inclusive. At 60 Hz this permits short holds of 9…27 ticks, long holds of 34…52 ticks, and onsets up to 13 ticks early/late; 14 ticks is outside .22 s.
- `activeHold` is null or `{index,direction,startedAt,duration}` describing only the player's actual latched direction and actual hold. It has no expected kind. `playerDirection` describes the current selected joystick direction or null. A renderer may show both fixed legal hold bands simultaneously; it must not reveal the expected band's identity in response mode.
- `feedback` is null or `{type,reason,index,at,expiresAt,holdDuration?}`. Types are `accepted`, `mistake`, `phrase-complete`, `replay`, or `cancel`; reasons describe an actual event, never future expected answers. `lastError` retains the most recent error reason. `message` is human feedback.
- `mistakes`, `maxMistakes=4`, `replaysRemaining=2`, `maxReplays=2`, `replayCount`, `acceptedCount` (lifetime actual accepted presses, including retried prefixes), `cancelCount`, `resumeCount`, and `requiresNeutral` are public counters/status.
- `tick`, `elapsed=tick/60`, `timeLimit=90`, `remaining=max(0,90-elapsed)`, `score`, `lostReason` (null, `mistakes`, `timeout`). `events[]` resets per `step` and has at most 32 entries. Only bounded pattern/phrase/timing arrays are stored.

## Rhythm, errors and ordinary controls

Each listening pass starts with .8 s lead-in, then notes start every 1.5 s. After the final listening beat, a 1.8 s visible count-in announces the first response onset. Correct phrases light their beacon; the next phrase starts after .8 s feedback. The final correctly released note lights the third beacon and wins. Normal three-phrase demonstrations take approximately 33/43/51 s before input/observation variance; there is no production minimum-duration or automatic-completion rule.

Joystick input `{x,y}` is clamped; max magnitude below .25 selects no direction, the larger absolute axis wins, and ties choose horizontal. A true A press latches this direction once. Moving the joystick during a hold does not change that note. The press must fall within the current unanswered note's onset ±.22 s and match its direction; a real release must then fall within that note's short or long duration range. Hold duration is release tick minus press tick. An `aPressed` pulse without `a` held has zero hold duration and cannot earn a note.

Early/late presses, wrong/missing direction, too-short release and overhold cause one mistake and discard the unfinished phrase's accepted prefix. No-input onset expiry causes one `missed` mistake after the inclusive late window. An overhold is rejected immediately after the expected maximum duration, even if A has not been released. A remains disarmed until neutral after a rejected held press; a held button cannot produce repeated mistakes every frame. A during `listen` or error/completion feedback is ignored. A pressed too early in count-in is a genuine early-response mistake. After an error, .8 s feedback is followed by listening to the same phrase again. The fourth mistake ends the round. Previously lit beacons remain lit.

B is an edge-triggered **paid replay**, with two credits for the entire round. In `listen`, `count-in` or `respond`, an available B consumes one credit, cancels any hold, clears the current phrase's accepted prefix, and starts listening to that same phrase again. It does not spend a mistake or stop the 90-second clock. B during feedback, or with no credits, is rejected with feedback and no state-progress effect. Holding B never repeats it. B takes priority over A on the same tick; a replay discards that A edge. Ordinary A timing processing follows otherwise. If replay is unavailable, it does not suppress an otherwise valid A.

## Input cancellation and pause

`input.cancelEpoch` is a nonnegative monotonic counter supplied by the shared runtime whenever it clears input (pause/disconnect/blur/start). Its first observed value only initializes tracking, without canceling a normal start. A later increment cancels an active hold without awarding or penalizing it, discards pending A/B edges, sets `requiresNeutral`, and ignores the cancellation tick's A/B edges. `input.cancelled=true` marks any cancellation-owned or synthetic-neutralized input sample. Every tick with that flag ignores A/B press/release judging and **cannot** clear the neutral gate, including multiple physics ticks from the same rendered frame. Only a subsequent normal non-cancelled sample with A actually neutral can rearm. The runtime quarantines physically held keyboard/phone buttons until actual release, rejects DOM autorepeat after clearing, and supplies this metadata; the model cannot infer physical key-up from an arbitrary false boolean alone. The active timer continues during cancellation-owned ticks.

During `respond` or `count-in`, cancellation preserves the accepted prefix and all lit beacons, then reanchors the current unanswered onset to `elapsed+1.8`, followed by the remaining onsets at 1.5 s intervals. It enters a new visible count-in with **no direction/kind cue**, no replay-credit cost and no mistake. During listening or feedback, cancellation does not restart or shift the mode's scheduled clock. Shared pause/disconnection freezes the model by not calling `step`; active elapsed resumes only when ticks resume. The re-count-in itself uses active time. Repeated cancellation never stops the finite 90-second active clock.

## Terminal priority and score

At each tick the 90-second deadline is checked before cancellation, replay or note evaluation; an action at the deadline cannot rescue an expired round. A mistake that reaches four loses immediately. Each response note can be accepted only once, and only a completed phrase lights a beacon.

During play, score is `litCount*1000 + acceptedPrefix*100`, where the prefix contributes only if the current phrase's beacon is not lit. Replaying or failing an unfinished prefix removes its provisional points; cancellation preserves them. Winning adds `floor(remaining)*10 + replaysRemaining*100 + (maxMistakes-mistakes)*50`. Losing retains the current earned/provisional score after the applicable error reset. Terminal `step` calls freeze all fields. A fresh create/retry has independent arrays, no held input, no canceled or replayed state, zero progress and the full initial budgets.
